About the Brand

In the founder’s own words:

Psychotic Love isn’t about perfection.

It’s about the scars, the healing, the chaos, and the beauty of still loving despite everything.

Every stitch, every crack, every distressed graphic represents real emotion.

“Love isn’t perfect. Neither are we.”
Instagram TikTok

Psychotic Love exists to give a voice to the emotions people bury. Through clothing, we turn scars into stories and pain into self expression. The name starts to make sense as a contrast: the “psychotic” side represents the chaos, pain, thoughts, and battles people hide, while “love” represents healing, passion, self-expression, and choosing to keep going. A reminder that even through chaos, pain, and imperfections, there is still love, growth, and purpose.
